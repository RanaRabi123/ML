Data leakage finding

Customers correlates ~0.82 with Sales but is measured on the same day as Sales — not known in advance. Including it as a feature raises R² to 0.891, which looks better but cannot be achieved in real forecasting. The 0.393 R² above is the honest, deployable number.

Business insights
  • Promotions are the strongest lever known in advance — running a promo lifts average sales from 5,929 to 8,228, a ~39% increase.
  • Store identity matters a lot — individual stores vary substantially in baseline sales due to location, size, and local demographics.
  • Calendar effects are real but secondary — day of week, week of year, and month collectively help forecast staffing and inventory needs.
  • Customers should not be used as a forecasting input — it isn't known ahead of a sales day, even though it's the strongest raw correlate of Sales.
  • Report performance honestly — the leak-free pipeline explains ~39% of test variance; this is the number to share with stakeholders, not the inflated ~89%.
